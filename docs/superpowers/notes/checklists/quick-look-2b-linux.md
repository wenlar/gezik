# Quick look 2b on Linux: manual checks

Numbering starts at 520 (left room after 502). Results go to `../linux-test-results.md`.

520. **Layout.** In a folder of pictures, select a small one (under 400 px) and press Space: it shows at its own size, centred, on a darker stage (dark theme) or a light one (light theme). A large photo fits the window. One bar under it: the name in bold, "JPEG image · 2.1 MB · 4000 × 3000" after it, − + Fit, Hex, ⓘ and Open.
521. **Previous / next.** Move the mouse over the picture: ‹ and › show at the sides and "12 / 56" at the top; they go about 2 s after the mouse rests, stay while it is on ‹ or ›, and go when it leaves the window. Click ›: the next item shows and the list's selection moves (in the grid and in the list). On the first item ‹ is dimmed, on the last ›.
522. **Info.** Click ⓘ: a column opens on the right with the name, type, Size, Dimensions, Modified, Created, Location and the photo's properties, scrolling when long. Close quick look with Space, open it again: the column is still open. ⓘ again closes it.
523. **Open.** Click Open: the file opens in its app and quick look closes. On a folder Open goes into it.
524. **Zoom from 100%.** On a small picture press +: the zoom goes from 100% to 125% without a jump. Fit brings it back to 100%, not blown up. A PDF still fills the window when fitted.
525. **Text and video.** A text file: Text | Hex, Wrap and the line count in the bar, the name still elides first when the window is narrow. A video: ▶, the seek bar, time, Mute and volume.
526. **Pane unchanged.** The preview pane (Alt+P) looks as before: its picture, buttons and facts under it; a small picture there is not blown up either.
